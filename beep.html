Beep's backpack

New pieces

A shelf of fresh pieces: drag one in — it copies itself, so the shelf never runs out. Whatever it replaces moves to your spare tiles. Drop a purple operation on a piece to wrap it. Statements drop straight into the program — new jumps grab the nearest flag. Changed your mind? Drop the copy on Trash.

Spare tiles

Trash

Tap a purple = statement to open it. Drag a piece to move it — every drop swaps, so no slot is ever empty. Tap a piece for its menu: change its value, or wrap it in a + / −. Tap the sign to flip it or wrap the whole group. Drag an operand to Trash (or the spare tiles) to collapse it back. Whole statements ride the same rules: drag a block here to keep it, to Trash to delete it, or tap its grip for duplicate / stash / delete. Deleting a flag that wires point at leaves those jumps pointing at nothing — Beep will stop, confused, if he tries one.

Run it

Play fast to play with ← →. Then Step or Watch to see how it works: yellow blocks ask a question, and if the answer is yes, Beep jumps along the wire to a flag. Wires on the right jump down to little jobs; wires on the left jump back up. Drag any block or flag to rearrange the program and see what breaks!
