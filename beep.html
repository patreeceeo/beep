← → move the paddle

Play fast to play with ← →. Then Step through it one block at a time to see how it works: yellow blocks ask a question, and if the answer is yes, Beep jumps along the wire to a flag. Wires on the right jump down to little jobs; wires on the left jump back up. Drag any block or flag to rearrange the program and see what breaks!

Beep's backpack

Beep's backpack is a stack of pouches. The bottom one is the world — the numbers that run the game. Every visit adds a pouch on top and every return takes one away, so a pouch is where Beep is, what he knows there, and where he goes back to. Numbers inside a pouch are notes. When Beep looks up a name he checks his own pouch first, then the ones below, then the world — and writing works the same way, so a note keeps living wherever it already is. Want your own copy? Use new note. The ghost pouch on top is what you are packing for the next visit: pack puts a parcel in it, visit carries it along, and unpack takes parcels out of the pouch you are in. Whatever a visit packs and does not unpack comes back to whoever called it.

New pieces

A shelf of fresh pieces: drag one in — it copies itself, so the shelf never runs out. Whatever it replaces moves to your spare tiles. Drop a purple operation on a piece to wrap it. Statements drop straight into the program — new jumps grab the nearest flag. Changed your mind? Drop the copy on Trash.

Spare tiles

Trash

Tap a purple = statement to open it. Drag a piece to move it — every drop swaps, so no slot is ever empty. Tap a piece for its menu: change its value, or wrap it in a + / −. Tap the sign to flip it or wrap the whole group. Drag an operand to Trash (or the spare tiles) to collapse it back. Whole statements ride the same rules: drag a block here to keep it, to Trash to delete it, or tap its grip for duplicate / stash / delete. Deleting a flag that wires point at leaves those jumps pointing at nothing — Beep will stop, confused, if he tries one.
